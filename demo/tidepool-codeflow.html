drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · click a building to trace it
How to read this: the repo is laid out like an engine, inputs to outputs. Execution enters at the intake (entrypoints, gold beacons) on the left; each stage is what the previous stage imports; everything settles into the core on the right, the modules the whole program leans on. Every connection carries two currents: gold pulses are logic, calls flowing from importer to imported, the way the arrowheads point; blue droplets are data, results returning the opposite way. Each building is a file (taller = more lines, color = language), grouped and tagged by folder. Click any building to trace what it depends on (cyan) and what depends on it (orange). The mode menu switches five views: the engine schematic (this one; phases run left to right and ribbons heat up from blue to red as they go deeper), an orbit constellation of folder clusters you can tumble to any angle, a circuit board of folder chips and copper traces, an arc skyline of import arcs, and a rotatable 3d flow pipeline where view: folder rivers aggregates flow folder-to-folder.
